# Smart Event Platform — Contributor Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, supervisors, and leaders of the **Smart Event Management & Marketplace Platform** project pledge to make participation in our project and community a harassment-free, productive, and rewarding experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

---

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive and productive environment include:

- **Demonstrating Empathy and Kindness:** Treating fellow teammates, collaborators, and supervisors with mutual respect and consideration.
- **Respecting Technical Experience Diversity:** Acknowledging that team members have different areas of specialization (Frontend, Backend, AI, UI/UX, Documentation) and levels of familiarity with particular tools.
- **Constructive & Evidence-Based Feedback:** Offering critique focused objectively on architectural specifications, code quality, and testing standards rather than personal attributes.
- **Graceful Acceptance of Constructive Feedback:** Viewing peer reviews and supervisor advice as opportunities for individual and team growth.
- **Accountability & Reliability:** Taking responsibility for assigned tasks, communicating blockers proactively, and honoring team commitments and academic deadlines.
- **Focusing on What Is Best for the Overall System:** Prioritizing unified architectural cohesion, system stability, and user experience over individual preferences.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- The use of sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information, such as physical or email addresses, without their explicit permission.
- Dismissive attitudes toward teammates seeking help or asking questions about unfamiliar technologies.
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional or academic software engineering setting.

---

## Enforcement Responsibilities

The project leadership, supervisors, and maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior that they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Maintainers have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and other contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct, and will communicate reasons for moderation decisions when appropriate.

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces—including local team meetings, Git commit histories, GitHub pull requests and issues, project documentation, group chats, and academic presentation sessions.

---

## Conflict Resolution & Reporting

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior should be reported directly to the Project Supervisors or designated Team Lead in a private and confidential manner. All reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.

All project leaders and supervisors are obligated to respect the privacy and security of the reporter of any incident.
